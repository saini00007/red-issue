About

Established in 2010, Infinity Capital is a privately held investment group headquartered in the Kingdom of Bahrain. Infinity Capital deploys its proprietary sources of capital through a diversified portfolio which includes real estate, private equity, venture capital and financial securities across numerous geographies.

The group continues to expand its focus on investment and value creation opportunities with particular interest in development, hospitality, lifestyle, entertainment and sports assets, amongst others that offer long-term growth and sustainable returns.
