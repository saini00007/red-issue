## Gap analysis (mechanism-level)
1. **Coordinator decision layer bought, not yet trusted**: Abhedi already has boss+PlanGate — the XBOW differentiator is that their coordinator demonstrably drives sequencing; Abhedi's is default-OFF and only reorders work. Mechanism needed: per-tick plan → claim-bias binding with measured conversion (objective→cells closed).
2. **Proof artifact as product**: all three competitors lead with reproducible exploit evidence; Abhedi's oracle stack exists but defaults OFF and is bypassed at finalize promote (finalize.py:442-449). Buyer-visible gap until the gates are the DEFAULT post-validation (D5).
3. **Browser-backed client proof**: XBOW publicly advertises steerable headless browser proof; Abhedi's browser stops at crawl + canary instrumentation; no agent-actable session/state machinery (WS-6 design).
4. **Whitespace reinforcement**: third-party-audited benchmark + transparent evidence format is unclaimed market space (COMPARISON.md:119, "independent verification biggest unmet need") — aligns exactly with the platform's oracle-disposes thesis.
