# Competitor Research — AI Pentesting / Attack Surface Management

This folder contains competitive intelligence on three AI-driven offensive-security startups — XBOW, FireCompass, and Escape — compiled September 26, 2026 by reconciling each company's own website content with independent OSINT (news coverage, funding databases, academic papers, review sites, and critic commentary). Each company has its own subfolder with a synthesized dossier, a raw external-research file, a structured facts file, and the individual crawled web pages used as source material; a top-level `COMPARISON.md` pulls all three together into a single cross-company analysis.

- **[COMPARISON.md](./COMPARISON.md)** — Cross-company comparison: executive summary, a feature-by-feature matrix, positioning, funding/scale, research credibility, key numbers, strengths/gaps, and market takeaways for a potential new entrant.
