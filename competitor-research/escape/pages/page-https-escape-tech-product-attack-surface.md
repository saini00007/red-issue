# Escape Attack Surface Management

## Summary
This is Escape's product page for its Attack Surface Management (ASM) offering. It positions the product against "legacy EASM tools" that only find hosts and ports, claiming Escape instead discovers APIs, SPAs, and AI applications across distributed organizations in real time, mapped down to the application layer. The page combines marketing headlines and stats with customer testimonials, partner/customer logos, feature descriptions, and links to related research and resources.

## Full content

### Attack Surface Management, Reinvented
Your attack surface. Fully mapped to the application layer.

Legacy EASM tools identify hosts and ports, while Escape discovers APIs, SPAs, and AI applications across distributed organizations in real-time. The platform claims to map everything engineers build and expose at the application layer.

### Core capabilities
- **Agentless API Discovery** — Maps exposed and internal APIs including shadow APIs without requiring agents.
- **AI Proof of Exploit & Remediation** — Delivers code fixes and proof-of-exploit traces.
- **Automated Asset Mapping** — Routes findings to code owners and product teams.
- **Integration Capabilities** — Connects with the Wiz risk platform and developer tools.
