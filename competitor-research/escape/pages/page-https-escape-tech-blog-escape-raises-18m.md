# Escape raises $18M Series A to replace legacy scanners with AI agent-driven discovery, pentesting, and remediation

## Summary
Escape, an offensive security engineering platform, announced an $18 million Series A funding round led by Balderton Capital, with participation from Uncorrelated Ventures, IRIS, and Y Combinator, dated March 10, 2026. The post, authored by co-founders Tristan Kalos and Antoine Carossio, describes Escape's platform for continuous attack surface discovery, business-logic-aware DAST, and AI-driven pentesting, aimed at understaffed security teams. It cites usage by 2,000+ security teams globally and includes customer statistics and a testimonial from Visma, plus a quote from Balderton Capital partner Suranga Chandratillake.

### Company Mission
Escape develops an offensive security engineering platform designed to help understaffed security teams discover, test, and fix vulnerabilities continuously within engineering workflows. The platform automates attack surface discovery, security testing, and contextual remediation.

### Product Offerings
1. Attack Surface Management – continuous visibility from code to cloud
2. Business-Logic-Aware DAST – replaces legacy scanning with intelligent testing
3. AI Pentesting – continuous, automated penetration testing at scale

### Quote from Investor
