## Summary
XBOW's pricing page does not publish specific price figures; instead it positions pricing as usage-based and "scoped to your environment," tied to the scale of the customer's application portfolio rather than a fixed annual contract. It highlights continuous (not point-in-time) coverage, validated findings with proof-of-concept exploits, and availability through AWS, Google, Oracle, and Microsoft cloud marketplaces.
