# Leadership | XBOW

## Summary
XBOW's Leadership page introduces the company's executive team under the headline "Meet the Pioneers of Autonomous Offensive Security," positioning XBOW as built by the creators of GitHub Copilot and GitHub Advanced Security. It profiles eight leaders (CEO, CISO, CRO, Chief Product Officer, Head of AI, Head of Engineering, Head of Finance & BizOps, and a regional GM for South Korea) with headshots and links to their LinkedIn/GitHub/X profiles, then closes with a standard demo call-to-action.
