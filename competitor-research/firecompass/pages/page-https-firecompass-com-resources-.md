## Features / claims mentioned
- Agentic AI platform performs autonomous/agentic web application and mobile application penetration testing.
- Continuous Automated Red Teaming (CART) and External Attack Surface Management (EASM) offered as a single SaaS platform.
- Daily risk port scanning and adversary emulation through "multiple Attack Playbooks."
- Prioritized risk findings with real-time alerts for faster detection and remediation.
- Agentic AI-powered web application pentesting claims 99%+ coverage and <2% false positives with real-world attack-path validation (per whitepaper teaser).
- "Harness engineering" is positioned as what turns LLM reasoning into validated exploits (vs. LLMs alone, which the company argues only "generate text, not exploits").
- Autonomous AI pentesting can chain minor findings into multi-stage attack paths (account takeover, remote code execution), per a case study teaser.
- Continuous pen testing claimed to scale across large (2,000+ app) enterprise portfolios, reducing per-test cost and false-positive rate.
- Compliance mapping/alignment offered for SEBI (India's securities regulator) AI Circular and CERT-In's AI-threat guidelines.
- "Mythos" is referenced repeatedly as a named theme/framework in multiple whitepapers/handbooks about AI-accelerated threats and board communication — appears to be FireCompass's branding for an AI-security narrative or benchmark context (not otherwise defined on this page).
- FireCompass ranked #1 AI on HackerOne (per site banner) — consistent with other pages' claim of ranking in HackerOne's "Top 3."
- Company described as "An EC Council Ecosystem Company."
