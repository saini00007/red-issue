# FireCompass — External Research Profile

*Compiled from third-party/outside sources only (news outlets, funding databases, review sites, social media, job boards, press releases, and analyst mentions). FireCompass's own website (firecompass.com) was intentionally excluded — it is being crawled separately.*

*Research date: September 26, 2026*

## Executive Summary

FireCompass is a Bengaluru (India) / Boston (US)-headquartered cybersecurity startup, founded in 2019, that sells an "agentic AI" offensive-security platform combining External Attack Surface Management (EASM), Continuous Automated Red Teaming (CART), Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM), and AI-driven automated penetration testing for web apps, APIs, and network infrastructure. It was co-founded by serial cybersecurity entrepreneur **Bikash Barai** (previously founder of iViZ, acquired by Cigital/Synopsys) along with **Arnab Chattopadhayay**, **Priyanka Aash** (co-founder of CISO Platform), and reportedly **Nilanjan De** and **Ravi Mishra** (the latter two appear to have since left). The company has raised roughly **$30–30.5M** across seed (2021, NetApp Excellerator), Series A ($7M, Feb 2023, led by Cervin Ventures and Athera Venture Partners), and a $20M corporate/venture round from **EC-Council** in September 2025. It holds a USPTO patent for its CART methodology, claims Fortune 500 customers, has been recognized in multiple Gartner Hype Cycles/Market Guides, GigaOm Radar (Leader, 2024 & 2025), and as an IDC Innovator, and made news in mid-2026 for running an AI agent that reached top-3 rankings on live HackerOne bug-bounty leaderboards on a $5,000/month budget. Employee headcount is estimated at roughly 85–92 people (per Tracxn/other people-data aggregators) versus ~11–50 shown on some LinkedIn snapshots — sources disagree. Public reviews (G2, Gartner Peer Insights, Glassdoor) are generally positive but sparse in volume, with recurring complaints about false positives, UI polish, and onboarding. Competitively, third-party comparison articles (mostly published by competitors Escape.tech and cybersecurity blogs) position FireCompass as broad in surface coverage (web+API+network chaining) versus XBOW (web-app-focused, benchmark-driven) and Escape (API-security specialist) — see Section 11.

**Biggest gaps/unknowns:** exact current valuation (redacted in Tracxn), precise total headcount (sources conflict: 11–50 per LinkedIn vs. ~89–92 per Tracxn/Crustdata), a definitive founder list (Nilanjan De and Ravi Mishra's current status is unclear/likely departed), detailed pricing tiers (no public price list; only third-party estimate ranges), and independent verification of internally-generated benchmark claims (XBEN 104/104, HackerOne top-3) since these largely trace back to FireCompass's own press releases and self-authored blog posts, even when republished by outside outlets (Yahoo Finance, Business Standard, etc.).
